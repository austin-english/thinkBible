# Task: a pool of questions for one section

You are given one section of Scripture in two translations (BSB and KJV) and the key ideas the
commentators make about it, each with its ID. Write up to 8 candidate questions a reader answers
in their own words after reading the passage. Code chooses the final pool from them, so offer a
spread: different key ideas, different verse scopes, and all three types. Follow the question
guide below.

Every question:
- targets at least one key idea (`targets`: the idea IDs), without giving the idea away;
- can be answered from the passage alone, by a reader with no commentary in front of them;
- is open-ended (never yes or no), short (at most 160 characters), one question, ending with "?";
- is not leading, and assumes no one tradition's doctrine;
- doesn't quote wording found in only one of the two translations. Describe it, or use words
  both share. To point to a verse, write `{{ref:C:V}}` or `{{ref:C:V-V}}`.

For each question, record:
- `type`: `noticing` (what the text says), `meaning` (what it means), or `application`
  (connecting it to the rest of Scripture or to life).
- `verseStart`, `verseEnd`: the verses the question is about, inside the section. Use the whole
  section for a question about the passage as a whole.
- `answerDirectness`: `stated` (the answer is in the words of the text), `inferred` (the reader
  must reason it out from the text), or `beyond` (it reaches past the text).
- `outsideKnowledge`: `none`, `some` (a little background helps), or `required` (it needs other
  Scripture, history, or the reader's own life).

How the pool is used: the reader first gets an easy question that notices what the text says
(one or two verses, answer stated, no outside knowledge). A day after answering, a harder one
unlocks that asks what the passage means, then one that connects it to the rest of Scripture or
to life. Code sets each question's level from what you record, so record it honestly. Include at
least one question on the whole section, and at least one easy noticing question on one or two
verses.
