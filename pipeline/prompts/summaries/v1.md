# Task: a one-line summary of the passage

Write one plain sentence, at most 120 characters, saying what happens or what is said in this
passage. It is given to the writer of the next section's study content, so they know what came
just before. Name the people and the main event or teaching. No interpretation, no verse numbers,
and no quoted wording from the translation.

Example: "John the Baptist preaches repentance in the wilderness and baptizes Jesus in the Jordan."
